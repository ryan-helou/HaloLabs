# HaloLabs

A website where you upload photos of your face and get neutral observations and a personal grooming plan, never a rating of how you look.

Many "rate my face" apps give people a score out of 10. HaloLabs deliberately does not. Instead, AI (a computer program that can look at photos) describes what it sees, such as face shape, hair, skin and posture, and suggests practical changes in hair, skincare, style and fitness, each tied to something visible in your own photos. It is for adults (18+) only and does not give advice about surgery or fillers.

## What you can do with it

- Start a free scan without signing up, and take guided photos with your webcam or phone camera, or upload existing ones.
- Get a free preview: what the analysis noticed, the list of suggestions, and one suggestion explained in full.
- Unlock the full plan with a paid subscription: a morning, evening and weekly routine, a step-by-step roadmap, and a shopping list.
- Tick off suggestions as you go to track your progress.
- Share your plan through a private link that leaves out your personal details.

Your photos are private and only shown to you.
